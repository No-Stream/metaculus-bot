## Code Review Results

This is the `metaculus-bot` repo (your Metaculus forecasting bot), branch `mantic-competition`, reviewing commit `e37b8ae` "Forecast date questions first class and optimize the pipeline for Mantic" against its parent. The change makes date questions a first-class question type by riding them through the numeric pipeline on an epoch-seconds adapter, adds a pre-spend tournament permission gate for Mantic (the Crucible competition on competitions.mantic.com, a Metaculus fork), adds Mantic-gated prompt clauses, and widens the closed-bound clamp tolerance for coarse grids. Fourteen reviewers plus a verifier pass produced 59 surviving findings, which dedupe to 38.

### Verdict: NEEDS WORK

Eight findings are fix-eligible. One of them (F1) changes what the bot publishes today on both platforms, so it is the reason for the verdict rather than a nit.

### Important (should fix)

- **[F1]** `metaculus_bot/numeric/bounds_clamping.py:32, 50, 68, 97` The one number returned by `calculate_bounds_buffer` does three jobs and only one of them wanted the widening (confidence: 95) — concurred: correctness, completeness, grounding, concern-1, concern-2, codex-bugs
  - Problem: the buffer is the acceptance tolerance, the distance inside the bound at which the accepted value is PLACED, and the proximity window for the heavy-clamping WARNING. Now that it is at least one grid bin, a value just outside a closed bound is relocated a whole bin inside the range, and because the strict-ordering pass then runs left to right it drags every percentile declared inside that bin up with it. Four reviewers reproduced it independently on the real sanitizer: on the recorded 12-bin Mantic date question 6.3 points of probability leave the day the member declared (one repro published 0.9456 of the mass in the wrong day); on a 22-point discrete question a declared 20.7 lands on the bin 19/20 edge, which right-closed bucketing assigns to the lower bin; and on an ordinary 201-point Metaculus question over [0, 20000] four declared percentiles all publish at 100.0, three of which were never out of range at all. The diagnostic job is separately wrong: on a 13-point date grid the window is a whole day at each end, so a legitimate, fully in-range forecast logs `Heavy bound clamping ... clamped_to_upper=69%` with nothing clamped.
  - Fix: keep `calculate_bounds_buffer` as the tolerance only; add a `_boundary_inset(question)` standoff (the same epsilon `_spread_cluster_values` already uses) and clamp to `bound +/- inset`; have the diagnostic measure against that inset and drop its now-unused `buffer` parameter. Five tests pin the old target and need re-pinning, not the three the individual findings named.
  - Note: three verifiers wrote that this fix "already landed on disk" while they worked. It has not. The tree is clean at `e37b8ae` and lines 50 and 68 still write `bound +/- buffer`, so apply it in full.
- **[F2]** `metaculus_bot/numeric/cluster_processing.py:159` A fully concentrated declaration is dropped as a unit mismatch on the grids where it is valid (confidence: 85) — concurred: grounding

On a grid whose bins ARE the outcome space that reasoning does not transfer: "100% on 2026-09-16" is expressible and, under Mantic's scoring, a high-value answer. Measured: 13 identical values drop the member, 12-of-13 identical publishes 0.96 of the mass in the correct bin. Then keep the record honest: the NUMERIC_DEGENERATE_DECLARATION line hardcodes `spread_applied=false`, so move the emission below the spread and render the real value; and update the two docstrings plus the comment that say the collapse is withheld by design.
- **[F3]** `metaculus_bot/prompts.py:1427` The date schema note invites the declaration the pipeline withholds (confidence: 82) — concurred: correctness, concern-1
