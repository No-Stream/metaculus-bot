2. **Polling is stale relative to the question’s opening**

   - It is therefore **[PRE-WINDOW — occurred before question open, cannot itself satisfy the criteria]**, even though coverage was published after **30 June 2026**.

3. **No poll methodology**
   - Without sample size, error margin, field dates, undecided treatment, or full candidate breakdown, the **55%** number cannot be rigorously compared with official election results or with later polling.

4. **Elite predictions are not equivalent to empirical estimates**
   - Kasune’s “six million votes,” Mwale’s **75 percent**, and Chanda’s assertions about **70%**, **80%**, **90%**, and **95%** are political or expert commentary.
   - They differ sharply and are not backed in the supplied research by methodology or measured voter data.

   - The opposition stresses high living costs, hunger, unemployment, and a claimed **$6.5 billion** reserve balance.
