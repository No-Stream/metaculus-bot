**Zambia's Bishops Appeal for Peace Ahead of August Elections**
Ahead of the 13 August Presidential and General Elections in Zambia, the Catholic Bishops of Zambia issued a Pastoral Statement urging violence-free, peaceful, and civil electoral campaigns. The Bishops highlighted that Zambia is one of the few African nations to avoid civil war since its independence in 1964 and has maintained peaceful transfers of power. The Electoral Commission of Zambia has confirmed 12 candidates for the election, including incumbent President Hakainde Hichilema (United Party for National Development), Brian Mundubile (National Reconciliation Party for Unity and Prosperity), Harry Kalaba (Citizens First), and Fred M'membe (Socialist Party). The Bishops stated, 'Peace is not the absence of conflict but the fruit of justice, mutual respect, and solidarity among citizens,' and warned that 'ambiguity may cost lives.' They explicitly called for the rejection of political violence, intimidation, and the use of social media to spread hatred or fabricated claims. The statement also urged young people not to be used as instruments of violence. President Hakainde Hichilema welcomed the message, with his Chief Communication Specialist, Clayson Hamasaka, confirming that the government remains committed to safeguarding peace and ensuring a free, fair, transparent, and credible election.
