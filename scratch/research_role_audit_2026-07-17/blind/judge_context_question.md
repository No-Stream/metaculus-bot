# Question (Metaculus Q44229)

What percentage of votes will Hakainde Hichilema receive in the first round of Zambia's 2026 presidential elections?

## Resolution criteria
This question will resolve as the percentage of votes that Hakainde Hichilema receives in the first round of the Zambian presidential elections scheduled to be held on August 13, 2026, according to official results published by the [Electoral Commission of Zambia](https://www.elections.org.zm).

## Fine print
If results are not available before September 1, 2026, for any reason, or if Hichilema dies or otherwise ceases to be a presidential candidate for any reason this question will be **annulled**.&#x20;
