## Research summary

**The 2026 election has not yet taken place.** Zambia’s Electoral Commission has scheduled the first ballot for **August 13, 2026**, with results due to be declared on August 17. [A: official — Electoral Commission of Zambia] ([elections.org.zm](https://www.elections.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2026-Elections-Timetable-14-August-2025-30-days.pdf))

There is **no reliable, independently verified forecast that establishes one precise expected vote share** for Hakainde Hichilema. The available evidence is:

- **Zambia Election Research Network (ZERN) survey:** In a nationally structured telephone panel conducted from **June–November 2025**, **55%** of respondents said they planned to vote for Hichilema, 13% for an opposition party, and 32% either did not know or declined to answer. The survey had **1,497 respondents**, used stratified random-digit dialing, and its authors caution that the long fieldwork period and telephone-based sampling limit its precision as a snapshot of opinion on July 18, 2026. [A: primary academic survey — UCT Centre for Social Science Research/ZERN] ([humanities.uct.ac.za](https://humanities.uct.ac.za/cssr/idcppa/publication/bp-03-26))

- **A separate University of Zambia-affiliated poll reported in December 2025:** Secondary reports described Hichilema at approximately **60%**, with the opposition combined at 35% and head-to-head results between 58% and 63%. However, I could not locate a publicly available full methodology or primary release for this poll, so it should be treated as weaker evidence. [C: secondary report] ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Zambian_general_election?utm_source=[MODEL-REDACTED]))

- **Reuters’ July 9, 2026 reporting:** Reuters cited the ZERN result as showing **55% intending to vote for Hichilema**, while noting that the survey preceded Brian Mundubile’s campaign launch. Reuters described Hichilema as favored but said the opposition should not be dismissed. [B: Reuters] ([marketscreener.com](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/zambia-election-will-put-hichilema-s-economic-record-to-the-test-ce7f5ed9d18cf723))

- **Prediction markets:** Polymarket’s market concerns **who wins**, including a possible runoff, rather than Hichilema’s first-round vote percentage. Its explanatory page cited polling averages of **55–63%**, but this is an aggregator’s summary and not an independently verified vote-share forecast. Another market-analysis page showed Hichilema’s win probability fluctuating substantially and explicitly warned that trading volume was thin. [C: prediction-market aggregator] ([polymarket.com](https://polymarket.com/pl/event/zambia-presidential-election-winner-20260604205247322/will-hakainde-hichilema-win-the-2026-zambian-presidential-elections-20260604205342549))

### Historical reference class

- In the **2021 presidential election**, Hichilema received **2,852,348 votes**, or **59.02% of valid votes**, according to the African Union’s election-observation report summarizing official ECZ results. Turnout was 70.61%. [A: official results summarized by AU] ([au.int](https://au.int/sites/default/files/newsevents/reports/40695-rp-FINAL_REPORT_OF_THE_AUEOM_TO_ZAMBIA_2021_GENERAL_ELECTIONS_-_5_NOV.-1.pdf?utm_source=[MODEL-REDACTED]))
- In **2016**, Hichilema received **47.63%**, losing narrowly to Edgar Lungu; in 2021 he therefore gained roughly 11 percentage points. [A: election-observation report] ([parliament.gov.zm](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/articles/final_report_eu_eom_zambia_3.pdf?utm_source=[MODEL-REDACTED]))

### Bottom line

The strongest directly documented pre-election indicator puts Hichilema at **55% stated voting intention**, while a less transparent poll reported **about 60%**. The available evidence therefore supports a description of his current measured support as **roughly mid-to-high 50s**, with substantial uncertainty due to undecided voters, the age of the best-documented survey, and possible changes following opposition mobilization. **A precise factual percentage for his eventual first-round result cannot yet be established.**
