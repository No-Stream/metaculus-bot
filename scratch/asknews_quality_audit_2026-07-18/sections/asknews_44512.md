#### Historical Context

### Australia’s historical Commonwealth Games dominance
**Source:** Mirror, published **July 08, 2026**  
- The Daily Mirror guide says Australia has won **1,001 gold medals** and topped the medal table **13 times** since the Commonwealth Games began in Hamilton, Canada, in **1930**.
- It also states that at the **2022 Birmingham Games**, **61 per cent of the 280 gold medals** were won by Australia, England, Canada, or India.
- The article notes that **43 of the 72 participating nations and territories** secured a podium finish in Birmingham 2022.
- Other historical notes in the piece:
  - Scotland achieved their best **“away”** performance.
  - Wales secured **eight golds** for their third-best showing.
  - Guernsey won **a silver and a bronze**.

**Relevance:** Establishes Australia as a historically dominant Commonwealth Games nation, which is important context for expectations around 2026.

#### Recent Developments

### Kaylee McKeown withdraws from the Games
This is the most important late-breaking development.

**Source:** CNA, published **July 10, 2026 06:03 AM**  
- Australian swimmer Kaylee McKeown is withdrawing from the Pan Pacific Championships (**August 12-15 in Irvine, California**) and the Commonwealth Games (**July 23-August 2 in Glasgow**) due to glandular fever.
- She is **24-year-old**.
- She holds a record **10 individual backstroke titles** and world records in the **50m and 200m backstroke**.
- McKeown said:
  - **“What I thought a few months ago was the flu has turned out to be my body fighting glandular fever,”**
  - and she fears pushing herself could lead to chronic fatigue.
  - **“I am gutted to medically withdraw,”**
  - and she will be **“M.I.A.”** for the upcoming events.
- She previously won **four Commonwealth Games gold medals** in Birmingham in **2022**.
- She is focusing on recovery and intends to be ready for the **2028 Los Angeles Olympics**.

**Source:** The Globe and Mail, published **July 10, 2026 12:45 AM**  
- Describes McKeown as a **five-time Olympic swimming gold medalist**.
- Says she won the **100- and 200-metre events at both the Tokyo Olympics and the Paris Games in 2024**.
- Confirms she has medically withdrawn due to glandular fever.
- Says the Commonwealth Games are scheduled for **July 23 to Aug. 2**, with swimming starting **July 24**.
- Says the Pan Pacific Championships are set for **Aug. 12-15**.

**Source:** easternriverinachronicle.com.au, published **July 10, 2026 12:48 AM**  
- Says McKeown had aimed to repeat her **four gold medals** from the **2022 Birmingham Games**.
- Says she is a **five-time Olympic gold medallist**.
- Says Australia’s team includes **59 swimmers**.
- Says Australia aims to secure top spot on the medal table for the **15th time in 23 Games**.
- Says Australia previously won **65 medals, including 25 golds**, at the Birmingham Games.
- Quotes Petria Thomas, Australian team chef de mission, expressing confidence McKeown will recover in time for the **2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games**.

**Source:** Sky News Australia, published **July 09, 2026 09:53 PM**  
- Confirms McKeown withdrew due to glandular fever and chronic fatigue.
- Says she had wanted to compete despite illness and had hoped to push through because Glasgow was significant: her sister Taylor McKeown won gold in **2014**.
- Says her withdrawal is a blow to the Australian team.
- Repeats that Australia aims for top spot on the medal table for the **15th time in 23 Games**.
- Petria Thomas is quoted supporting McKeown’s recovery.

**Relevance:** McKeown was one of Australia’s strongest gold-medal prospects in swimming; her withdrawal likely reduces Australia’s gold ceiling, though the exact impact is not quantified in the sources.
