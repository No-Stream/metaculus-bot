The [XXIII Commonwealth Games](https://www.glasgow2026.com/about/about-us) is a multi-sport event held in Glasgow, Scotland, that is scheduled to take place from July 23 to August 2, 2026. This will be the fourth time Scotland will host the Games and will be the first Games held since the death of Queen Elizabeth II and the accession of King Charles III as [Head of the Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_the_Commonwealth "Head of the Commonwealth").

Since the first Games in 1930, Australia have dominated the medal table, winning the most gold, silver, bronze and overall medals of any country. However, their total medal count has been inconsistent: Australia won 67 gold medals in 2022, 80 gold medals in 2018, and only 49 gold medals in 2014.

The 23rd edition of the Games will include 3,000 athletes from 74 nations and territories and will involve [10 sports](https://www.glasgow2026.com/news/4152372/glasgow-2026-sport-programme) and six para sports, "striking a balance between ensuring the event has a multi-sport feel and the need to manage financial and operational risk." There will be 215 gold medals available across the [10](https://www.glasgow2026.com/schedule/day-by-day)[ days](https://www.glasgow2026.com/schedule/day-by-day) of the event.
